Account Creation Guide

Learn how to register as a Creator or Investor and choose between Individual or Company accounts on the Northern Investment Portal.

Understanding Account Types

Choose the account type that matches your role in the investment ecosystem

Creator

Project & Proposal Submitter

A Creator is an individual or organization that submits project proposals, uploads business ideas, and seeks funding opportunities through the portal.

Key Responsibilities:

  • Create and submit detailed project proposals
  • Upload supporting documents and project files
  • Manage and edit submitted proposals
  • Wait for approval from authorized reviewers
  • Respond to investor inquiries and follow-ups

Ideal for: Entrepreneurs, Project Developers, Business Owners, NGOs

Investor

Funding & Investment Partner

An Investor is an individual or organization that reviews approved proposals and provides funding for viable projects submitted by Creators.

Key Responsibilities:

  • Browse approved and verified investment opportunities
  • Click the Invest button to fund selected projects
  • Access detailed project information and financial data
  • Track investments and portfolio performance
  • Communicate directly with project creators

Ideal for: Angel Investors, Venture Capitalists, Banks, Corporate Investors

3-Step Registration Process

Simple and straightforward account creation process

1

Select Your Account Type

Choose between Creator or Investor

Based on your role in the investment ecosystem, select either Creator (if you want to submit projects) or Investor (if you want to fund projects).

2

Choose Registration Type

Select Individual or Company

Both Creators and Investors can register as:

Individual Account

Personal account for solo entrepreneurs or individual investors

Company Account

Business/organization account for companies, NGOs, or institutions

3

Complete Registration

Enter details and submit application

Fill in the required information based on your selected account type and registration category:

Required Information:

  • Personal/Company details
  • Contact information
  • Verification documents
  • Secure password setup

After Registration:

  • Account verification (24-48 hours)
  • Email confirmation
  • Access to portal features

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about account registration

Can I switch between Creator and Investor accounts?

Yes, but you need to create separate accounts for each role. One email can be used for multiple account types, but they function independently with different permissions and features.

What documents do I need for Company registration?

For Company accounts, you'll need: Business Registration Certificate, Company Director's ID, Tax Registration Number, and authorized representative documents.

How long does account verification take?

Individual accounts are typically verified within 24 hours. Company accounts may take 24-48 hours due to additional document verification requirements.

Can I upgrade from Individual to Company account?

Yes, you can request an account type upgrade through the portal's support system. You'll need to submit company documents and go through a verification process.

Is there a fee for account creation?

No, account creation on the Northern Investment Portal is completely free for both Creators and Investors. We believe in removing barriers to investment opportunities.

What if I need help during registration?

Our support team is available 24/7. You can contact us at support@nisportal.gov.lk or call +94 21 222 2222 for immediate assistance.
