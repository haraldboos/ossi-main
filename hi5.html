What is the Northern Province?

The Northern Province of Sri Lanka is a culturally rich and historically significant region, known for its vibrant communities, beautiful coastline, and emerging economic opportunities. It is a key area for investment and development in sectors like agriculture, tourism, ICT, and renewable energy.
