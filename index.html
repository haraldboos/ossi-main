Strategic Advantage

Why Northern Sri Lanka?

Discover why Northern Sri Lanka is emerging as one of South Asia’s most promising and strategic investment destinations.

  • Northern Sri Lanka is rapidly becoming a new frontier for sustainable investment, serving as a gateway to South Asian markets and located just 24 km from India.
  • Its strategic location on major global maritime trade routes enhances international connectivity, supported by 8,800 sq km of underutilized land and rich natural resources.
  • The Northern Province contributes nearly 30% of Sri Lanka’s fisheries production, strengthening growth in marine, aquaculture, and export industries.
  • Upcoming industrial zones, tourism hubs, and MSME clusters will accelerate regional economic expansion and job creation.
  • Investors benefit from tax incentives, capital allowances, public–private partnerships, and 30–40% lower operating and labor costs.
  • With full foreign ownership, multi-year tax holidays, priority government backing, and 24/7 investor support, Northern Sri Lanka stands as a high-impact investment destination.

Everything you need to know about investing in Northern Sri Lanka

The Northern Investment Summit 2026 is a premier investment platform showcasing opportunities across all five districts of Northern Sri Lanka. It brings together global investors, government stakeholders, and local enterprises to drive sustainable economic development through strategic partnerships and investments.
You can access the One-Stop Shop for Investment (OSSI) portal by clicking the "Access OSSI Portal" button in the OSSI section above. The portal provides comprehensive information on investment opportunities, project proposals, regulatory frameworks, and connects you directly with key stakeholders across all districts.
The Northern Province offers diverse investment opportunities across 9 main sectors: Agriculture, Industry & Trade, Infrastructure, Regional Development, Human Resource Development, Environment Management, Social Protection, Communication Technology & Research, and Governance. Each sector has multiple sub-sectors for targeted investments.
Investors benefit from comprehensive incentives including tax holidays, capital allowances, full foreign ownership, strategic public-private partnerships, and streamlined approval processes. The region offers 30-40% lower operating costs compared to other South Asian investment destinations, with dedicated 24/7 investor support.
You can explore district-specific opportunities through our interactive investment map in the Districts section. Simply hover over any district for quick information, or click to view detailed investment profiles including ongoing developments, key projects, and contact information for local investment authorities.
Investment proposals can be submitted through the OSSI portal, where you'll find a structured submission process. The portal guides you through required documentation, connects you with relevant authorities, and provides real-time tracking of your proposal status. For complex projects, our investment facilitation team provides personalized assistance.
