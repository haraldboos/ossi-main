Proposal Upload Guide

Step‑by‑step instructions to submit your project proposal on the OSSI portal.

Before you start: Creator account required

You must have a Creator account on the OSSI portal. If you haven't created one yet, please refer to the Account Creation Guide (also linked in the navigation bar). Once your creator account is active, you can access the proposal creation link.

STEP 1

Access the proposal creation form

Click the image to enlarge – first, read all guidelines carefully.

After clicking “New Proposal”, you will see the guidelines. Read every guideline before filling the form.

STEP 2

Fill the proposal form

Refer to the numbered fields below (image: guid/5.png)

Field definitions

  • 1
    Project title – Clear, concise name reflecting core idea (e.g., "Solar-powered Irrigation System for Kilinochchi").
  • 2
    Exclusive summary – Short pitch (2-3 sentences) summarising problem, solution, and expected impact.
  • 3
    Sector – Main category (Agriculture, Technology, Infrastructure, etc.) from dropdown list.
  • 4
    Subsector – Specific area within sector (e.g., under Agriculture: "Crop Production", "Agri-tech").
  • 5
    Project location – Northern Province (preselected).
  • 6
    District – Jaffna, Kilinochchi, Mullaitivu, Mannar, or Vavuniya.
  • 7
    DS division – Divisional Secretariat division (e.g., "Valikamam South").
  • 8
    GN division – Grama Niladhari division, smallest administrative unit.

After selecting subsector, location fields (5‑8) appear dynamically.

STEP 3

Upload required documents

Mandatory files (marked with *)

  • 1. Project proposal document * – Detailed description (problem, methodology, timeline, budget).
  • 2. Financial statements / projections * – Income/expense projections, balance sheets, funding breakdown.
  • 3. Concept note / pitch deck * – Visual summary (10-15 slides).
  • 4. Creator identification * – NIC/passport (individuals) or company registration (organizations).

All fields with * are required. Other documents are optional but recommended (e.g., letters of support, environmental clearances).

Note: You must upload every mandatory file. The system will not accept the proposal if any * field is missing.

STEP 4

Upload video & presentation

Proposal video

Short presentation video (max 50 MB) – introduce your team, demonstrate the product, or explain the project's value. Keep it under 3 minutes. (mp4, mov, etc.)

Presentation deck

Slides summarizing your project (max 20 MB) – cover problem, solution, market, financials, and team. (PPT, PDF, Keynote)

Video must be directly related to the proposal. Both files are mandatory unless stated otherwise in the guidelines.

STEP 5

Previous submission & final step

(Image shows question 11 and status options)

11 Previous submission check

You will be asked: “Have you submitted this proposal before?”

  • Yes – you previously submitted this exact proposal to any platform or agency.
  • No – this is the first time you submit this proposal.

If you answer Yes, the system requires additional details.

Additional details for "Yes"
Where was it submitted?

Mention the platform or agency (e.g., "OSSI portal 2025", "District Secretariat", "Other funding call").

Current status of that proposal
  • Accepted – proposal was approved (funded or shortlisted).
  • Under evaluation – still in review process.
  • Rejected – was not approved.
If rejected – reason

Provide a brief reason if known (e.g., "Budget constraints", "Incomplete documentation", "Feasibility concerns"). This helps reviewers understand the context.

12

Submit proposal – what happens next

After clicking “Submit”, the system sends a confirmation email to your registered address.

Your proposal enters the review queue. Track its status (pending, under evaluation, approved, changes requested) from your creator dashboard.

You receive email notifications when status changes or reviewers leave feedback.

Confirmation email Dashboard tracking Status notifications

Ready to upload your proposal?

Make sure you have all mandatory documents and follow the numbered fields.
